Reflection

Summary

As a student researcher and leader devoted to advancing the field of quantum cybersecurity and building engineering spaces, I’ve come to see the value in a people first approach. My work is focused on building up the people to solve complex problems and better the world. Through developing products that increase the access of critical hardware for cybersecurity education, I hope to reach students across the world to bring interest in quantum cybersecurity to top tech talent. By developing student councils and competitions across the country, I hope to build spaces where these students can showcase their projects, build crucial leadership skills, and network with similarly interested researchers and peers.

Research

Through my work as an undergraduate researcher, I have worked on projects to utilize agentic systems to provide an evolving defense system for future quantum computers. I have also worked to develop educational tools such as a quantum key distributor to build the next generation of quantum talent. I have developed my professional communication skills through internships in interdisciplinary labs, where I have learned how to present technical work and show its value to non-technical shareholders.

Leadership

I have also worked extensively in leadership roles, both local and national, to develop a strong engineering identity in students across the country. By founding MissouriHacks, I have worked to increase the reach of our computer science program and provide resources for K-12 students to learn how to utilize AI to develop projects in today’s quickly shifting technological world. By building engineering student councils across the country, I have worked to provide spaces for students to grow as a leader and make a change in their community.

Conclusion

I hope to build the future of quantum cybersecurity by developing products that can help the next generation of tech talent find their field. Throughout my career, I have focused extensively on building tools, systems, and spaces for educators and students alike. By helping people from all backgrounds find their love for engineering, I hope to be a part in advancing the future of technology.

Ashton Wooster 10/18/26
